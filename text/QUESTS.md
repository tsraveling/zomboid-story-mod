## Zombie Meat (first)

CDC will recommend bringing the ham radio with you.

### 1: Get the meat

- Interact w/ corpse w/ a knife or other blade to get the get meat interaction. Get two samples.

## Sample Comparison (tri)

### 1: Collect stuff

- Get a zombie brain
    - needs a hatchet
- Get a sample of Velvet from any police station
    - lootable
- Get a soil sample from the March Ridge crash site
    - use spade in rect on map
- Get a microscope

Once collected, call CDC. Get a Doctor++ skill as CDC explains the work. -> 2.

### 2: Do analysis

With all three samples in inventory, interact with microscope. perform analysis. Takes a while. teaches some more skill. Sets understanding: -> 3

### 3: Report

Report your findings to CDC. Somehow, all of these things are related -- ancestors of the current Z strain.

## Junkie Body (junkie)

### 1. Find the body

Go to Muldraugh PD morgue. Body appears on floor. Examine and take sample. Narrative interaction -> 2

### 2. Analysis

Microscope again

### 3. Report

Call CDC

## March Ridge Scientist (march)

We know the chief scientist lived in March Ridge, and we know where his house is.

### 1: Search Chief Scientist's House

- house at https://projectzomboidmap.com/#9970x13101x1739

Upstairs, there's his body, self-inflicted gunshot to the head, with a note on it that points to the aide in the military apartments. "I'm just going to pick up my X at home" on answering machine.

### 2. Find the aide

Aide is a military-uniformed zombie in the [northwest apartment of the top floor of the military apartments](https://projectzomboidmap.com/#10054x12617x684x3), with the book in his inventory.

### 3. Read the book to the CDC

Interact with the radio; takes quite a while. You get some skill buffs from it.

You learn about the secret military base (`base`) and where the savant ("the bokor") lived (`methlab`).

## Military Base (base)

--- This quest walks you through the military base, several locations etc. At the end of it you have some hard drives that you remove from the mainframe, and you have to set up a computer at a broadcast tower to transmit.

## Meth Lab (methlab)

--- Dixie Trailer Park -> meth lab in the woods -> McCoy estate. We find out about the boss in prison, and that he was going to write a confession.

## Prison (prison)

--- Find the mob boss. Brutal combat.
