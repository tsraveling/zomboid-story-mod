## Sample Comparison (tri)

### 1: Collect stuff

- Get a zombie brain
    - needs a hatchet
- Get a sample of Velvet from any police station
    - lootable
- Get a soil sample from the March Ridge crash site
    - use spade in rect on map
- Get a microscope

Once collected, call CDC. Get a Doctor++ skill as CDC explains the work. -> 2.

### 2: Do analysis

With all three samples in inventory, interact with microscope. perform analysis. Takes a while. teaches some more skill. Sets understanding: -> 3

### 3: Report

Report your findings to CDC. Somehow, all of these things are related -- ancestors of the current Z strain.

# Mafia Thread

## Meth Lab (methlab)

--- Dixie Trailer Park -> meth lab in the woods -> McCoy estate. We find out about the boss in prison, and that he was going to write a confession. He'd worked it out with his lawyer and was due to deliver the day of the Event. The paperwork is still in there somewhere. It hints at the development of Velvet and that the facilities are much bigger than the little lab in the woods. He was sent to prison and has written a confession; maybe it's still there somewhere.

-> boss

## Prison (boss)

--- Find the mob boss. Brutal combat.

1. Our one lead is that the lawyer was staying at the prison motel. If we're lucky, he's still there.
    - Interact w front desk to figure out which room he's in
    - Briefcase is in the room. References but does not contain the confession. It's a whole thing. He was scared. His fellow inmate is a priest who helped him write it.
2. Go to the [prison library](https://projectzomboidmap.com/#7442x11756x574)
    - Search the library desk for a note indicating a specific book the boss kept reading.
    - Find that book on the shelves
    - Read it; the confession falls out.
    - Read confession to CDC.

-> mccoy
