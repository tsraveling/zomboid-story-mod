# Our Story

There was a nanotech superweapon. It kills its host, mimicing a biological agent. It then bootstraps the central nervous system. It is capable of extremely efficient carbon energy conversion; the idea was that it would eat and then use that carbon to build more nanotech substrate. It gathers energy from the sun, radiation, ambient heat and uses that to control the muscles. How it does this for so long is unknown, and perhaps unexpected to its designers. Might be quantum.

Players:

- The military. Developed the weapon under credible threat. Nominally to look for a cure; in fact as a WMD. Idea was that it could be keyed to specific genetic profiles. Tough luck, Sino-Americans. The mutations were unexpected.
- The Academy. Academics have been coming on contracts to LSU for some time now; actually serving as part of this initiative.
- The Mafia / Cartel. The origin of the thing was a new drug, created by an autistic savant. He wanted to understand people, made a drug for it; got manipulated into cooking for these assholes. Military caught wind of it, tried to figure out how to manage it. Brought in academics.

The Savant was an infamously detailed person. He kept a massive log of all of his work and couldn't function without it; this is the macguffin.

Events:

- The invention. The Savant once worked at LSU, but crippling mental illness drove him out. He had a house or apartment there, but ongoing events destituted him, and he ended up impoverished, barely hanging on. He needed his invention. He cooked meth near Muldraugh in exchange for access to the chemical lab. And there he cracked it.
- His new drug caught the attention of the cartels. They brought him to work for them in Louisville, in the Two Towers. With his new lab he could really cook. The drug was just a side effect -- his own healing was *so close*.
- Spiffo's is a cartel front, much like El Pollo Loco. The hot sauce thing was a mixup; a drug shipment disguised as hot sauce actually getting mixed in.
- The police caught a lead. Junkies acting very strangely. This isn't the military version yet -- they're not zombies but they are unusually controlled. and even dead they twitch for a *long* time (need a mortuary, maybe [sunset pines funeral home](https://projectzomboidmap.com/#13179x1539x192x2)).
- Military catches wind. Joint task force; cartel boss is sent to prison near rosewood; Savant is moved into the military research facility.
- A cartel manufacturer keeps trying to make new product; it's difficult, and causes some problems; namely that it is not limiting itself to the initial consumer. Something shifted. Nanotech is adjacent to life; it's not easy to control. Junkies start to transmit this strange "fading sickness" to others they contact, through bodily fluids, fights. This isn't just in Louisville, it's all over.
- Academics are flown in to help understand the Savant. Unfortunately one of them has a huge gambling problem. To cover this he offers to sell the discovery to the Chinese. In going after it, he leaves a critical door unlocked (Jurassic Park style). He's at the Irvington Racetrack when the shit hits the fan a few weeks later.
- The Savant figures something out, but only when his work for the military reaches a critical mass. 
- A military insider, a closet fascist, tries to target a specific racial minority with it by releasing it into the water supply. He is much too dumb. The shit aerosolizes; that same horrible smell as was originally in the Spiffo's hot sauce.
- Up front this is a chemical weapon. But then it interacts with the new strain from the cartel, and something happens; it begins to self-replicate.
- The Savant is tasked with a cure, and manages to figure something out. But then as the cure is being transported to the airport for covert aerosolization (phones being shut off prior just in case), a raccoon causes a freak accident, and the truck crashes. Most of the cure washes out into the forest. It does *accidentally* protect a few people, including yourself. No hope for the damned, though.
- The shit hits the fan. Somewhat tragically, the Savant has become functional as a result of all of this, and realized she needs to figure out how to survive. Her grandfather is the last person still living in that abandoned down; she steals a vehicle and heads out there. Unfortunately he has already turned. She holes up in the old factory, and tries to start retrofitting the abandoned asylum into a research lab to reproduce the cure; and then she just fucking disappears.
- The final task is finding her Ledger, and then uploading it. A neutralizing agent can be synthesized, and those few million remaining humans will be immune. It won't kill the living dead, though. Get to the chopper.
